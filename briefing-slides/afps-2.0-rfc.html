RFC · problem

Four actions · strict precedence

The consequence decides the stop

1

Infer + proceed

Clear evidence, reversible choice, cheap correction.

2

Assume + proceed

Meaningful uncertainty; state it close to the work.

3

Decision checkpoint

Taste, selection, costly direction, or material low confidence.

4

Permission stop

Destructive, irreversible, public, paid, legal, privacy, security, or external.

Check from 4 → 1. A decision packet can never satisfy a permission stop.
